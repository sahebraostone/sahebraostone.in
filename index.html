High Performance Aggregates

Our Premium Product Line

Engineered, double-washed, and precision-graded stone products manufactured to exceed Indian Standard (IS) specifications for structural and infrastructure works.

Crushand

Particle Size: 0mm to 4.75mm

High-quality VSI Crushand cubical manufactured sand, double-washed to eliminate silt content for superior workability, high bonding strength, and flawless mortar plastering.

Primary Application: RCC Concrete, Plastering
Silt Content: < 3% (IS 383)

6 MM

Sieve Range: 6mm to 10mm

Precision-graded fine aggregate with an exceptional shape index, ideal for dense reinforced concrete, precast elements, roofing, and asphalt surface overlays.

Primary Application: RCC Slab, Precast Works
Crushing Value: < 18%

10 MM

Sieve Range: 10mm to 20mm

High-strength standard core aggregate widely used for heavy RCC structures, columns, beams, foundation footings, and robust bridge pier construction.

Primary Application: Beams, Columns, Footings
Specific Gravity: 2.65 - 2.75

20 MM

Sieve Range: 20mm to 40mm

Coarse heavy-duty angular stone aggregate designed for mass concrete foundations, road sub-base preparation, railway ballast, and heavy industrial flooring.

Primary Application: Mass Concrete, Base Course
Flakiness Index: < 15%

40 MM

Premixed Aggregate Base Course

Dense graded Crushand aggregate engineered for road base construction, providing high load-bearing capacity and rapid highway pavement stabilization.

Primary Application: National Highways
Compaction Efficiency: 98% MDD

GSB

Size: 150mm to 300mm+

Heavy-duty coarse aggregate and hard basalt stones designed for heavy fill applications, retaining walls, riverbank protection, and robust slope stabilization.

Primary Application: Retaining Walls, Riprap
Specific Gravity: 2.70
